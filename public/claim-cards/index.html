million-seconds-scale

calculation · high · original_calculation

One million seconds is about 11.57 days; one billion seconds is about 31.7 years using 365-day years.

http-get-safe-method

source_fact · high · paraphrase_with_citation

HTTP GET is defined as a safe method: it should not have the significance of taking an action other than retrieval.
